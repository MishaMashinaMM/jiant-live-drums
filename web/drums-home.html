JIANT LIVE
DRUMS v0.2

Live-oriented FM1 firmware with DRUM-X and DRUM-LAB (12 pads each), eight shared synthesis voices, per-track MIDI routing, and 32 assignable MIDI Learn destinations. DRUM-LAB also has eight kit-wide sound controls. The sequencer is disabled in this edition.

Connect to the FM1, edit patches, and save or restore complete backups.

Interactive manual

Inherited JIANT controls reference; some sequencer and drum pages differ in Drums.

Experimental: this Drums fork has passed host tests and a simulated Jiant Live → Drums upgrade, but has not yet been verified on physical FM1 hardware. Save a backup and arrange a recovery method before flashing. The editor/installer are fork-specific; do not use the original JIANT installer for this package.

Live behavior

Up to six independently routed tracks; overlapping or restarting drum hits; four configurable choke groups; MIDI CC fan-out across tracks. Existing drum sounds have their own independent editing controls.
